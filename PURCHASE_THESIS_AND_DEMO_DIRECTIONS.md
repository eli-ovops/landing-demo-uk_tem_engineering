# £600 purchase thesis and two research directions

## £600 Purchase Thesis

**Decision framing only:** User requested a £600 thesis for this UK research. It is not an approved price, proposal, customer-visible claim, or replacement for the project’s AU$800 product rules.

**Why an owner could see value:** TEM already has verifiable capability breadth—precision CNC machining, metal fabrication, engineering design/technical solutions, and complex project delivery—and a newly announced Gateshead trading location. The current site’s opening experience shows a generic industrial image and a contact form instead of making these buying-relevant facts legible. A well-scoped static concept could show a technical buyer in five seconds what TEM does, make the business feel specific through its real capability/language, then arrange services, credible proof, and one enquiry action in a sequence that supports a quote conversation.

**What this does not claim:** It does not prove additional leads, revenue, better rankings, buyer intent, or a confirmed £600 budget. It does not allow use of TEM photos, logo, names, address, testimonial, or staff imagery in a Demo.

## Direction A — “Capability, from workshop to delivery”

| Element | Research direction |
| --- | --- |
| Design read | A high-confidence industrial capability story for an engineer or procurement contact who must quickly route a manufacturing enquiry. |
| Hero composition | One cleared, authentic workshop/process image only; overlaid specific capability label and a single “Discuss a project” / quote-oriented route. If a TEM asset cannot be cleared, use a new non-representational original engineering-concept image that cannot be mistaken for TEM’s premises, staff, machine, or finished work. |
| Real fact mapping | CNC machining, metal fabrication, engineering design/technical solutions, project management, Gateshead location—each rechecked at production time from official source. |
| Service cut | 1) Precision CNC machining, 2) metal fabrication, 3) design-to-delivery/project support. Do not list unverified equipment, tolerances, or materials. |
| Bottom 40% | A factual “how a project moves” reassurance sequence (brief → manufacture → delivery) only if the process statements are verified; then a simple quote/contact action. |
| Mobile | Short non-clipping capability label, single hero image, stacked service lanes, CTA visible before any cookie/consent overlay. |
| Motion | `MOTION_INTENSITY 1`: no autoplay; optional reduced-motion-safe image/text reveal only after separate authorisation. |
| Commercial mechanism | Moves from what TEM does → why a buyer can trust the path → clear next step. |

## Direction B — “The engineering brief, made legible”

| Element | Research direction |
| --- | --- |
| Design read | A calmer, spec-led B2B presentation focused on reducing ambiguity for procurement or engineering teams. |
| Hero composition | Structured dark/light editorial grid around one cleared detail image or original non-representational material/process concept; key message is a verified three-part capability line rather than a generic slogan. |
| Real fact mapping | Same verified capability set, but expressed as a concise “machining / fabrication / project delivery” briefing frame. |
| Service cut | Three stacked service briefs that each state what TEM is publicly evidenced to offer, not fabricated numerical claims. |
| Bottom 40% | “When to talk to TEM” / appropriate project types only if re-verified, followed by location and direct enquiry action; no invented case studies or customer logos. |
| Mobile | Horizontal grid becomes a strict vertical reading order; every fact remains visible without a side-scroll or oversized display type. |
| Motion | `MOTION_INTENSITY 0–1`: static priority; no automatic motion needed. |
| Commercial mechanism | Makes the company easier to qualify before contact, particularly when the visitor arrives with a technical problem rather than a product SKU. |

## Direction choice

**Recommend Direction A** if independently accepted and separately authorised. It most directly corrects the current site’s gap while retaining the authentic industrial character indicated by the live hero. Direction B is the better alternative if cleared visual assets remain unavailable or the decision-maker wants a more procurement-oriented tone.

Neither direction may be implemented until: independent candidate acceptance; explicit Demo authorisation; a PRE_CODE_DECISION; rights/privacy decisions; and the then-current client facts are rechecked.
