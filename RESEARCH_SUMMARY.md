## Why a £600 concept could be commercially legible

The site already has a real industrial image and a recognisable company name, but it fails to quickly explain the breadth of the service offer or provide a quote-oriented path. A one-page direction could make the established capabilities visible in the sequence a technical buyer needs: what TEM makes; relevant machining/fabrication/design capability; evidence of delivery approach; then a direct quote enquiry path. This is a commercial-presentation hypothesis, not a claim about conversion, revenue, or purchase intent.

- **Reason to continue:** official current move announcement, a compact end-to-end engineering offer, public `sales@` route, and weak current mobile presentation.
